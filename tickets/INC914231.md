## 1. Issue Description

User's laptop screen remained black and would not power on, while the power LED was **slowly blinking on and off**. Standard presses of the power button produced no system response. The user was completely unable to work ahead of an important meeting.

## 2. Investigation & Triage

- Slowly blinking power LED indicated the system was receiving power but stuck in an unrecoverable **deep ACPI sleep state** (Modern Standby/S3 hang), not a dead motherboard or power failure.
- Standard power button triggers failed to wake the system due to frozen firmware/embedded controller states.

---

## 3. Resolution Steps

1. Instructed the user via **Team Chat** to disconnect all external attachments:
   - Docking station
   - AC power charger
   - External monitors
   - USB peripherals
2. Guided the user through a **hard power reset** — hold the power button for a full **30 seconds** to force a flea power drain until all LEDs and fans went completely dark.
3. User pressed the power button once to initiate a clean cold boot.
4. System successfully POSTed, booted into the OS, and displayed normal desktop functionality.

## 4. Root Cause & KB Notes

**Root Cause:** Unrecoverable deep ACPI sleep state (frozen firmware/embedded controller) prevented system wake. Standard power button triggers failed.

**KB Article:** KB-1056

**Quick Resolution Checklist:**
1. Confirm power LED is slowly blinking (not completely unlit — would indicate total power loss).
2. Unplug all peripherals, external displays, docking station, and AC adapter.
3. Press and hold the power button for a full **30 seconds** until all diagnostic LEDs turn off.
4. Press power button once to cold boot and confirm normal screen initialization.
