## 4. Root Cause & KB Notes

**Root Cause:** Corrupted or out-of-sync MFA verification token mechanism on the user's registered mobile device. An MFA reset cleared the stale authentication methods and forced re-enrollment of the primary authenticator device, restoring login access.

**KB Article:** KB-1088

**Quick Resolution Checklist:**
1. Open **Directory Services** and locate the impacted account by searching the username or UPN.
2. Select **Manage → Authentication** to inspect registered verification methods and MFA enrollment state.
3. Click **Reset MFA** to clear stale authentication methods and trigger a re-enrollment flow.
4. Instruct the user to attempt a fresh sign-in and complete the visual setup prompt to register their authenticator app.
5. Verify successful authentication and confirm full access recovery with the user.
6. Update ticket remediation logs and close the incident.
