## 4. Root Cause & KB Notes

**Root Cause:** Scheduled employee offboarding (HR Director approved). The account was disabled, active licenses revoked, security group memberships removed, and hardware assets disassociated per the offboarding checklist to ensure no unauthorized post-departure access remained.

**KB Article:** KB-1085

**Quick Resolution Checklist:**
1. Open **Directory** and search for the target employee by UPN or username.
2. Under **Manage → Authentication**, click **Disable Account** to block interactive logins immediately.
3. Access **Manage → Licenses** and remove all active product licenses (e.g., Office 365 / Enterprise Suites) to free allocated seats.
4. Access **Manage → Groups** and remove all distribution and security group memberships to prevent unauthorized permissions or mail routing.
5. Open **Asset Management**, search by user name, and update assigned devices to **Unassigned / returned inventory** status.
6. Log all remediation actions in the ticket system and notify the requesting HR representative.
